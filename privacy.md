# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 4, 2026_

**My People** ("the app") is a personal-use application. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

## Who uses this app

The app is single-user. The only user is the operator who installs and authorizes it. The app is not offered to, marketed to, or installed by any other person or organization.

## What data the app accesses

When you authorize the app via Google Sign-In, the app may access the following Google account data on your behalf:

- **Gmail** (`gmail.modify`, `gmail.send`) — read message contents and metadata, send messages on your behalf, and apply labels.
- **Google Calendar** (`calendar`, `calendar.events`) — read your calendar events and create, update, or delete events.

The app does not request access to Drive files, Contacts, Photos, or any Google service not listed above.

## How the data is used

Data is used solely to provide the in-app features described on the [home page](/) — reminding the user about important contacts, surfacing relevant email, and managing calendar events.

The app does **not**:

- Share, sell, or transfer Google user data to any third party.
- Use Google user data to serve advertising.
- Use Google user data for any purpose other than the user-facing features described.
- Train AI/ML models on Google user data.

## How the data is stored

- Authentication tokens are stored locally on the operator's own hardware.
- Email content and calendar events fetched by the app are processed in memory or stored locally for the operator's own reference; nothing is transmitted to third-party services beyond Google's own APIs.
- The app runs on a private server controlled by the operator. There is no cloud backend.

## Data retention and deletion

The operator can revoke the app's access at any time by visiting the [Google Account permissions page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) and removing "My People". Once access is revoked, the app can no longer read or modify the account. Any local data cached by the app can be deleted by removing it from the operator's local storage.

## Limited use disclosure

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
